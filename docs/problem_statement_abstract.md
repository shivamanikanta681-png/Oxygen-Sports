# Review 1: Problem Statement & Project Abstract

## Problem Statement
When Oxygen Sports manages equipment donations and sponsorships given to local tournaments and schools manually, it leads to operational gaps. Without a digital tracker, details of the sponsored events, exact items donated (such as kits or gear), and agreed-upon brand visibility (such as banners or jersey logos) are scattered across random spreadsheets, paper logs, and WhatsApp chats. This lack of structure leads to administrative delays and errors, such as duplicate donations or unfulfilled visibility agreements. Crucially, management has no central database to review visibility results, turning sponsorship investments into an unmeasured cost rather than a tool for building brand equity. Consequently, Oxygen Sports loses valuable opportunities to calculate and optimize their return on investment (ROI).

---

## Frontend Screens Scope
The frontend of the tracker consists of the following screens:
1. **Sports Event Sponsorship & Kit Donation Entry Form**:
   * *Purpose*: A validated input portal for staff to log new sponsorships or kit donations. It captures details of the recipient, date, items, values, and promised brand visibility types.
2. **Sports Event Sponsorship & Kit Donation Dashboard**:
   * *Purpose*: The main landing view showing a summarized table of all logged activities. Includes status badges, search options, filters by category, and sorting features for easy navigation.
3. **Detail & History View**:
   * *Purpose*: A comprehensive drawer or modal interface displaying the complete timeline, itemized details, and audit history of a selected sponsorship.
4. **Reports & Analytics Dashboard**:
   * *Purpose*: A visualization engine that translates raw data into meaningful trend charts (e.g., total spend, category distribution, and visibility success rate) to assist business decision-making.

---

## Project Abstract
The **Sports Event Sponsorship & Kit Donation Tracker** is a dedicated digital solution designed for Oxygen Sports to manage, track, and analyze sponsorships and equipment donations. The system will be used by retail/wholesale staff to enter donation requests, and by management to approve budgets and monitor brand visibility commitments. By replacing manual paperwork and scattered messaging with a structured relational database, the system automates status updates, provides real-time visibility metrics, and generates visual analytics on sponsorship ROI. Once built, this application will transform Oxygen Sports' sponsorship operations from an administrative burden into a strategic, data-driven initiative that builds measurable brand equity in the Hyderabad local sports ecosystem.
